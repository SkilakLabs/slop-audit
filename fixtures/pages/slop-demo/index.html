FAQ

Questions, answered honestly

Is my data used to train models?

No — never. Your data is yours, full stop. We do not train on it, we do not sell it, and we never will. Honest and transparent, with no fine print.

How is this different from a spreadsheet?

A spreadsheet can do anything — and silently does it wrong. Pulsora is deterministic by design: the same events always produce the same numbers, with a human approving every automated decision.

What does human-in-the-loop actually mean?

Every forecast, alert and suggested action is a draft until a person approves it. Nothing runs on its own — you stay in control, on your terms, at every step.

Do I need a credit card to start?

No credit card required for the free beta. Start free, explore the full console, and upgrade only when you are ready. No hidden fees, ever.

Can I export my data?

Yes — everything, at any time, in open formats. It is your data, not ours, and leaving should be as easy as arriving. Export your full event log and every report with a single click, no support ticket required.

How long does setup actually take?

Most teams are live the same afternoon. Connect a source, let Pulsora replay your history, and review the first draft of your forecast before the end of the day — with a human approving every step, never a black box.

Is this really deterministic?

Yes — genuinely. The same raw events always produce the same numbers, because every metric recomputes from source data rather than from a model's memory. It is not just reproducible, it is byte-for-byte reproducible.

What happens if I stop paying?

You keep every export you ever made. We hold nothing hostage — your data, your reports and your history are yours to take at any moment, in open formats, with no fine print and no surprise fees.

Who is Pulsora for?

Revenue leaders, operators and analysts at growing teams who are done arguing about whose dashboard is right. If you run a pipeline and want one honest number, Pulsora is built for you.
