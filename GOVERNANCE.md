# Governance

## Ownership

Skilak LLC owns the name "Slop Audit", the domain, the hosted deployment at
https://slopaudit.skilak.ai, package publishing, and the release process.

## Maintainers

Maintainers review and merge pull requests. Initially the maintainers are:

- Srinath Kannoju (GitHub `skinath`)

## How work happens

- Contributors propose changes through issues and pull requests.
- The calibration corpus in `fixtures/corpus.json` is the arbiter of rule
  disputes. When a rule change and a labelled site disagree, the site wins.
- No change to the scoring philosophy in AGENTS.md without a maintainer.
- Maintainers decide what merges.

## Becoming a maintainer

A contributor with a record of sustained, good pull requests may be invited to
become a maintainer.
