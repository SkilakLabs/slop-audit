# Security

## Reporting a vulnerability

Report vulnerabilities privately through GitHub's "Report a vulnerability"
(security advisories), not through public issues.

## Scope

- The scanner loads untrusted websites in headless Chromium. Sandbox escapes
  and SSRF against private networks (see `src/safety.js`) are in scope.
- The hosted app's rate limits are in scope.

## Bounties

There is no bounty. No rewards are paid for reports.
